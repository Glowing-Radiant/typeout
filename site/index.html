Exam patterns

PatternTimeStandard
SSC CHSL – English10 minutes35 words per minute (1,750 key depressions)
SSC CHSL – Hindi10 minutes30 words per minute (1,500 key depressions)
SSC CGL – DEST15 minutes2,000 key depressions

One word is counted as five key depressions, including spaces. The numbers follow recent SSC notices, but rules change: always read the notice for your exam, and use the Custom pattern or the error-limit setting to match it.

Exam simulation behaves like the SSC software: the passage is not highlighted and the test cannot be paused. Practice highlights the current word, marks wrong words as you go and lets you pause.

How marking works

When you finish, your text is lined up with the passage word by word. Each difference is a full or a half mistake.

MistakeExampleCounts as
Missed word“the cat sat” typed as “the sat”Full
Extra word“the cat” typed as “the big cat”Full
Wrong word or spelling“record” typed as “recrod”Full
Spacing“in the” typed as “inthe”, or “note book” for “notebook”Half
Capital letters“India” typed as “india”Half
Punctuation“however,” typed as “however”Half
Word order“very good” typed as “good very”Half
  • Errors = full mistakes + (half mistakes ÷ 2).
  • Error percentage = errors ÷ passage words you attempted × 100. Text after the last word you typed is not counted against you.
  • Gross speed = (key depressions ÷ 5) ÷ minutes.
  • Net speed = (key depressions ÷ 5 − errors) ÷ minutes.
  • KDPH = key depressions per hour.
  • A word cut off when the time runs out is not counted as a mistake.
  • You meet the standard when you reach the target key depressions in the time allowed and your error percentage is within the limit.

Typing in Hindi

Hindi passages are in Unicode, the same text you see in Mangal or Nirmala UI fonts. Type them with a Hindi keyboard on your device:

  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Add a language → Hindi. Add the Hindi Traditional keyboard (InScript). Switch with Windows+Space.
  • Remington (GAIL): install a Remington GAIL Unicode keyboard, such as the free Indic input tools published for government use, and choose it from the language bar.
  • macOS: System Settings → Keyboard → Input Sources → add Hindi – InScript.
  • Android and iPhone: add Hindi to your on-screen keyboard. An external keyboard gives a far more realistic test.

Accessibility

Type Out aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It works with the keyboard alone and with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver and TalkBack. You can change the theme, text size, spacing and typeface in Settings, and the reading aids let you hear the passage while you type.

If something does not work for you, please tell us so we can fix it.
